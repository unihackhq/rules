# Main Prize Judging Criteria

-   **✨ Polish, Design, and Execution**

    What does your prototype look like? Is it something that someone can understand and want to use? How does it balance aesthetics and accessibility? And most importantly, does it actually work as intended?

-   **💪 Technical Difficulty**

    Does the hack look technically interesting or challenging? How far does the team actually implement the functionality they claim to be doing? In other words: We don't want lies; and we don't want AI slop. 
    
-   **💡 Originality and Creativity**

    You've identified the problem - but how unique is your idea compared to other existing solutions? Reinventing the wheel is fine, as long as you know what you are reinventing.

-   **😲 Wow Factor** 

    Does it spark joy? Would this be something you use and show to your friends? Yes, this is totally subjective. But that’s what makes it fun.
